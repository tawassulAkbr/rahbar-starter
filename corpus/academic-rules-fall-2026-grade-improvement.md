# Academic Rules Addendum — Repetition of Passed Courses for Grade Improvement

Effective semester: Fall 2026

## 1. Improvement of grades

If a student repeats a course that has already been passed, the better of the
previous grade and the repeated-attempt grade is used in CGPA calculation.

## 2. Eligibility for repeating a passed course

Students who are not on academic warning may repeat an already passed course
only when the grade obtained in that course is below **C**. The course must
also have been offered to regular students of the respective batch within the
last one academic year.
